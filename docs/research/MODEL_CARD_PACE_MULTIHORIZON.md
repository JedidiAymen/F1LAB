# Model card — exploratory direct Race Pace horizons

**Status:** research only, not served or promoted. Sixty-six chronological fold bundles each fit median, q10 and q90 LightGBM regressors (198 estimators); seed 42 and existing bundle hyperparameters. Each uses lap number, reconstructed deltas from up to three recorded preceding laps, and timestamp-aligned weather/interval observations. No next-lap tyre/stint, eventual race-length, or retrospectively inferred clean/regime field is an input. Outputs are duration in seconds relative to a recorded-lap median anchor; raw quantiles can cross.

The task, dataset hashes, split policy, baselines, error distributions, failed or uncertain comparisons, model artifact hashes and exact commands are in [the experiment](RACE_PACE_MULTIHORIZON_PROTOCOL.md) and [dataset card](DATASET_CARD_PACE_MULTIHORIZON.md). Last-observed and median-anchor baselines are essential; at three and five laps the median anchor has lower observed MAE than LightGBM. At one lap no defensible pit-aware baseline was established. Selected populations, unknown provider latency, retrospective outcomes and previously inspected test races prevent unconditional live or generalization claims. Local-only trusted joblib files must not be loaded from user inputs.
