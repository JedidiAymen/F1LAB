Registered 2026-11-01 before this slice analysis. This stacks on PR #4 at `968013b937ed352ffb8ae2b5c3c66efd8582de14`; the 2026 sessions and original full-cohort results were already inspected. No claim of an untouched test, new model training, causal explanation or generalization is permitted.

Inputs: PR #4's verified `study.json`, `study.sha256`, `dataset_card.json`, and `predictions.parquet` with their recorded hashes. Preserve the primary all-completed-lap benchmark and original difficult cases. Calculate absolute and signed errors for last-completed-lap persistence, standard TCN seed 7, no-head-context TCN seed 42 (still anchored to persistence), and masked TCN seed 42. Summarize the complete 2026 test cohort and each event.

Predefined retrospective error slices are based on **future observed outcome**: absolute completed-lap duration change relative to the last completed lap ≤1 s, >1–5 s, and >5 s. They are diagnostic groupings only, not forecast-time features or operational labels. Also report three as-of context slices from the absolute difference between the most recent lap and median of three completed prior laps with the same thresholds; these inputs were available by the study cutoff under its retrospective timing assumptions. The thresholds are exploratory conventions and are not claimed to identify safety cars, pit stops, tyre effects, or a physical cause. Report counts, MAE, median AE, RMSE, mean signed error, model-minus-persistence MAE and 2,000 seed-42 bootstrap draws resampling session/driver clusters; a cluster is a driver in one observed event. These conditional intervals describe only the two fixed events and do not quantify new-circuit uncertainty. Small or empty slices are explicitly marked.

Compute train/validation/test distributions of the raw TCN residual correction relative to persistence, and its correlation with observed residuals only where each has nonzero variance. Treat training results as in-sample and validation/test as previously inspected; correlation does not establish causality. Save aggregate JSON and reproduce from original saved predictions; do not overwrite or alter their bytes. The goal is failure explanation, not a new forecasting model or completed Driver DNA 3.0.
